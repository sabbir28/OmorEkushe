2
Control Bar & Tray

Omor Ekushe launches directly to your top desktop overlay bar and minimizes cleanly into the Windows System Tray notification area.

3
Toggle Bangla Hotkey

Press Ctrl + Alt + B in any document, browser, or editor to switch between Bangla & English typing.
